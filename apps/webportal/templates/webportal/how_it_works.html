Recording from the device

Each employee is enrolled on the fingerprint device under a Biometric ID. The same ID is saved on their employee record, and that is how a scan is matched to a person. A scan whose ID doesn't belong to any active employee is ignored.

Live
The system stays connected to the device and records each scan within seconds. The employee's phone is notified right away.
Sync every {{ sync_minutes }} min
The system also re-reads the device's last 24 hours, so a scan the live connection missed is still picked up. Sync now on the Devices page does the same on demand.
  • Reading the same scan twice never records it twice.
  • If the system was offline, the device keeps the scans in its memory. When the system is back, the sync brings in the last 24 hours, and the days it missed are worked out automatically.
  • The system checks its own clock against an internet time source and corrects any drift, so a wrong server clock can't make someone late.
  • An employee is only judged from the day they were added to the system (or their hire date, if later). Days before that are never counted as absent.
How scans are sorted full-time

The device only records when someone scanned. The system decides what each scan means, using the day split at the midpoint, {{ g.midpoint|time:"g:i A" }}:

  • Scans at or before the midpoint belong to the morning, scans after it to the afternoon.
  • In each half, the first scan is the In and the last scan is the Out. Scans in between are ignored.
  • A scan within {{ duplicate_minutes }} minutes of the In is a double press, never the Out.
  • A scan at or after a session's end time can't be its In: a lone scan after hours counts as an Out with a missing In.

Manual entry

For when the device can't be used, for example during a brownout. On the Manual Entry page an administrator picks the employee and the punch type, and it is recorded at the current time, as if they had scanned.

  • Full-timers: AM In/Out, PM In/Out, and OT In/Out (overtime only if it is authorized for today). Part-timers: In/Out.
  • Duplicates are blocked: the same punch again within {{ duplicate_minutes }} minutes, or an Out within {{ duplicate_minutes }} minutes of its In, is rejected.
  • A manual punch keeps the type the administrator chose; the automatic sorting never changes it. It is labelled manual and records who entered it.
  • The employee is notified just like a device scan.
When each punch can be recorded

A full-timer's punch can only be recorded while it makes sense for the time of day, by the server's clock and the employee's own schedule. Outside its window the terminal shows the type crossed out with the reason, and the Record button is locked.

Day statuses

A full-time workday has two sessions, morning and afternoon. A session counts only when it has both its In and its Out.

PRESENT Both sessions complete, on time.
LATE Both sessions complete, at least one In was late.
HALF DAY One session complete, the other absent.
ABSENT Neither session complete.
IN PROGRESS Today, and the session hasn't ended yet. Nothing is judged until it ends.
INCOMPLETE Part-time only: a single scan, so 0 hours for that day.
Rest days and holidays

Days that aren't workdays (rest days) have no status and count for nothing. A holiday on a workday always counts, so Present + Late + Half day + Absent adds up to the workdays (see Holidays):

  • PRESENT A holiday off is a paid day: it counts as present, shown with the holiday's name.
  • ABSENT The employee had no scan on the nearest workday before or after it, so the holiday counts as absent instead, with its full lost time.
  • PRESENT A Special holiday with attendance required, and they completed the required session(s). If not, the day is judged like a normal workday.
  • Any status: they scanned on the holiday anyway, so it is judged like a normal workday.

Overtime

Overtime only counts when it's authorized beforehand. On the Overtime page an administrator authorizes an employee for a date, with an overtime start time.

  • On that day, scans at or after the overtime start are the OT In (first) and OT Out (last).
  • Overtime = OT Out minus the later of the OT In and the authorized start. Scanning in early doesn't add minutes.
  • Authorized but no OT scans: no overtime. Not authorized: staying late is just a later PM Out and never counts as overtime.
Part-time employees

Part-timers have no set hours. They can come in any day, weekends included, and are judged per week (Monday to Sunday) instead of per day.

  • The first scan of the day is the In and the last is the Out (the {{ duplicate_minutes }}-minute double-press rule applies). Hours for the day = Out minus In, with no break deducted.
  • A day with only one scan is INCOMPLETE and worth 0 hours.
  • They are never marked absent, late, undertime or overtime.
A week is met when they render at least {{ weekly_hours }} hours on at least {{ g.parttime_min_days }} different day{{ g.parttime_min_days|pluralize }}

Both numbers are set on the Schedule page's Part-time tab. An employee can have their own weekly hours, and only days with both an In and an Out count toward the days.

Met Hours and days both reached.
In progress The week isn't over yet.
Short The week ended without the hours, the days, or both. The badge says what was missing, e.g. "Short by 2h & 1 day".

Holidays

Holidays are set on the Holidays page. Both Regular and Special holidays are paid days off: no scan is needed, and a full-timer is counted present for the day, shown with the holiday's name. In reports the Present count says how many were holidays, e.g. "21 (incl. 1 holiday)". A recurring holiday repeats on the same date every year. Adding, editing or deleting a holiday re-calculates that date for everyone.

The day-before / day-after rule

A full-timer gets the holiday off only if they came to work on the nearest workday before and after it. If they had no scan at all on either of those days, the holiday is counted as absent too. This stops a holiday from being stretched into a longer leave.

  • Rest days and other holidays are skipped when finding the nearest workday.
  • Until the day after has happened, the employee gets the benefit of the doubt. The holiday is checked again once it has passed.
Special holiday with attendance required

For events such as a foundation day program, a Special holiday can require attendance for the morning, the afternoon, or both.

  • Complete the required session(s), with both In and Out, and the whole day counts as present: a full {{ day_hours }}, with no late, undertime or absence.
  • Otherwise the day is judged as a normal workday, so the missing sessions count as absent.
  • The day-before / day-after rule doesn't apply to these days.

Notifications & reports
  • Every recorded punch sends a notification to the employee's phone, e.g. "AM In recorded — 7:54 AM", or "AM In — 8:12 AM (Late by 7 min)".
  • Absences are notified ("Marked Absent") once the missed session has ended. After an outage, only yesterday is notified; older days are filled in quietly.
  • The mobile app shows the employee their own records, monthly summary and, for part-timers, their weekly hours, all calculated the same way as here.
  • Reports (PDF or Excel) use exactly the same calculations, per employee or department, for any date range.
